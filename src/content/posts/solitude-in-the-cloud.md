Working late at night with terminal screens glowing in a dark room has a meditative quality. The world outside is asleep, network traffic drops to baseline, and focus deepens.

There is peace in building systems that work silently in the background: git commits pushed to remote servers, workflows running in containers, static assets distributed across global edge caches.

> "True simplicity is not the absence of clutter, that's a consequence of simplicity. Simplicity is somehow essentially describing the purpose and place of an object."

Here's to the late night builders, the listeners of analog synths, and the quiet architectures that carry our ideas forward.
